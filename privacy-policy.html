Mirror Fold — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Mirror Fold ("the game") is developed by Jacob Sparks. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

The game itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Your level progress, settings, and purchase status are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and coarse location to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data usage documentation. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads on Android).

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Whether you own a purchase is recorded on your device and with your Google Play account.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
